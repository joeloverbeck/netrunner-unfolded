# Netrunner Unfolded

Vocabulary for playing Netrunner. Current rules meanings come from the source basis in [rules and content](docs/foundation/02-rules-and-content.md).

## Language

**Match**:
One Netrunner game between a Runner and a Corporation, with their identities and decks, from setup to a rules-defined game end. Ending a run does not by itself end the match.
_Avoid_: Scenario, encounter (for the whole game).

**Run**:
A Runner's attempt against a particular server, including its approach, encounter, movement, and applicable breach steps, until that run ends.

**Encounter**:
The part of a run in which the Runner deals with a particular rezzed piece of ICE and its abilities and subroutines. A run can contain several encounters.

**Rez**:
The Corp's activation of an installed unrezzed card by turning it faceup and paying the applicable rez costs. Installing a card for free does not by itself make a later rez free.

**Open information**:
Information about the game, its state, cards or abilities available to both players, such as public card faces and credit-pool counts.

**Hidden information**:
Information unavailable to one or both players, such as an opponent's hand or the order of either draw deck. Information can be known to one player while remaining hidden from the other.
